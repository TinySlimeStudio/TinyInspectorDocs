# Icon Viewer Window

{% hint style="info" %}
To open this editor in the toolbar, select <mark style="color:$primary;">Tools > Tiny Inspector > Icon Viewer</mark>
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
### Custom Icons

Support for custom icons will be introduced in an upcoming update, further enhancing the visual customization and usability of the editor.
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Zrzut ekranu 2026-02-22 002915.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The editor provides a complete preview of all icons available in Tiny Inspector, allowing you to browse their appearance at a glance. Hovering over any icon reveals its display name, making identification quick and intuitive.

To use an icon in your attributes, simply reference it with _<mark style="color:$primary;">**TinyIcon.\[DisplayName]**</mark>_ — a seamless way to enhance your tools with meaningful and recognizable visuals.

The editor also lets you click on any icon to preview its appearance in both light and dark themes — perfect for ensuring visual consistency across different editor skins. Additionally, you can view its resolution and unique ID, giving you full insight into the icon's technical specifications for more precise implementation.
