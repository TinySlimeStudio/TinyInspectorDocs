# Settings Window

{% hint style="info" %}
To open this editor in the toolbar, select <mark style="color:$primary;">Tools > Tiny Inspector > Settings</mark>
{% endhint %}

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Zrzut ekranu 2026-02-22 003538.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Tiny Inspector puts you in complete control, allowing you to tailor every aspect of the editor to match your personal workflow and visual preferences.

### <i class="fa-play">:play:</i> Start with the Basics

Begin by setting your default accent color — the primary highlight used across various attributes like [Switch](../attributes/misc-attributes/switch.md), [Enum Toggle](../attributes/selector-attributes/enum-toggle.md), and [Progress Bar](../attributes/number-attributes/progress-bar.md). This single change can dramatically transform the editor's look and feel to align with your branding or simply please your eye.

### <i class="fa-globe">:globe:</i> Global Attribute Control

Dive deeper into attribute-specific settings that apply universally across your entire project. Whether you prefer certain behaviors or visual presentations, these global adjustments ensure consistency without the need for repetitive manual tweaking. And with more advanced customization options planned for future updates, the possibilities will only continue to expand.

### <i class="fa-palette">:palette:</i> Fine-Tune Every Detail

For those who demand pixel-perfect precision, you have full command over all color-related elements within your attributes. Adjust saturation levels, enhance readability, modify contrast, or tweak any other color property until everything looks exactly as you envisioned. This level of detail ensures that your editor not only functions efficiently but also provides a visually comfortable and personalized workspace.
